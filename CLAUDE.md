# EmbryoGenesis Digital Rebuild: Knowledge Base

This repository holds the strategy, design direction, content and SEO specification that TechnoTaau Team is preparing for EmbryoGenesis (https://embryogenesis.in).

## Working preferences
- Address the client and sign all documents and proposals as **TechnoTaau Team** or **Jakhar Singh**.
- Read `docs/00-project-brief.md` before any task. It records the client's decisions, brand palette and compliance guardrails.
- The project is B2B-first (IVF clinics as collection-centre partners), English only, pan-India, and WordPress-based. TechnoTaau Team delivers design and content; the client's developer builds.
- Never publish test prices, superlatives ("best", "No. 1"), "patented" (use "patent application published"), testimonials, or therapy promotion. The therapy pages are held pending legal review.
- All medical content is reviewed by Prof. (Dr.) Pulakes Purkait and carries references and a disclaimer.

## Layout
- `brand/`: logo and palette reference.
- `docs/00-project-brief.md`: decisions and guardrails (source of truth).
- `docs/research/`: industry research, competitor/keyword/social landscape, current-site audit (15 Oct 2026).
- `docs/strategy/`: blueprint, SEO strategy, social strategy, brand voice.
- `content/`: page copy drafts for the WordPress build.
