Fix this week

These carry money, privacy or reputation risk today

  1. Admission form bank details. The form for the RBU, RBPU and BU campuses shows a bank account in the name of the SGSU Bhopal registrar. Please confirm the correct account for each campus.
  2. Workshop certificates are in Google. 17 certificate PDFs with participants' full names appear in search. Their "Scan to verify" QR code leads to a page that no longer exists. We will hide the PDFs from search and rebuild the verification page.
  3. Public test sites. A test site link sits on the RBU campus page. demo.ithsedu.in is open to the public, and iths.rbuchd.in shows a copy of an admissions page. Each needs a password or removal.
  4. Visible build note. The Shimla page shows a note saying its photos are temporary stock images.
  5. Wrong accreditation badge. The Shimla page shows a NAAC badge for Rayat Bahra University on a Bahra University page.
  6. Claims that need proof. Placement figures (95%, 100% and 0% on different pages), "India's first" and "the only institute in the world", and "15+ patents" (these should read "patent applications").

Your point 1translationalhealthscience.in for admissions and ads

Agreed. This website brings 44% of visits and 46% of enquiries and calls. The page used for paid search in 2026 was the top enquiry page on the site. We keep it as the admissions and ads website and make it safer and stronger.

  1. Protect what ranks. Every page with clicks keeps its web address. Any move needs a redirect we approve.
  2. Merge six weak posts into their programme pages: two nutrigenetics posts, two B.Tech posts and two older embryology and career posts. The useful content moves first, then the old address redirects.
  3. Win the near misses. Rewrite the titles of pages shown often but clicked rarely: "embryologist" (6,640 views), "bsc embryology" (5,107) and "stem cell and regenerative medicine course in india" (position 4.6, no clicks).
  4. Link the course levels. Show BSc, MSc, PhD and PG Diploma under each programme in the Courses menu. The PhD page has only two internal links, and the MSc Embryology page is not in Google at all.
  5. Refresh the season pages before April. The two "after 12th" guides rank first and second on Google for searches like "ivf courses after 12th".
  6. Ads: brand and PG/PhD search from February, with the peak May to August. Target students aged 18+ and parents only, with tracking fixed first.

People search for the university and the city, so those go first in page titles. Each campus page then gets:

  • the full address with PIN, the campus phone and WhatsApp, and a map;
  • real photos, hostel and travel information;
  • fees and FAQs;
  • its own Google Business Profile, named as on the campus signage;
  • a link from the university's own programme page.

Your point 3ithsedu.in, now and as the future university website

Agreed. ithsedu.in becomes the official voice of the institute. It never copies admission content. If ITHS later becomes a university, the old website moves into it cleanly.

ithsedu.in Official institute site

  • About ITHS, leadership, faculty
  • Partner universities
  • Research: publications, patent applications with numbers
  • Achievements, news and events
  • Media kit, careers, contact, policies

Never: programme details, fees, admission forms. Its "Programmes & Admissions" button goes to the admissions website.

translationalhealthscience.in Admissions and ads

  • All programmes and course levels
  • All four campus pages
  • Eligibility, fees, ITHS-SET scholarship
  • Apply and enquiry forms, ad landing pages
  • Career and course guides

Never: institute news. That moves to ithsedu.in at launch, together with Careers.

Brand searches will move. Today the old homepage takes searches for "ITHS", and they bring enquiries. Once ithsedu.in is live, Google will likely show it first. So ithsedu.in's home page carries an "Admissions 2027–28" band at the top, which sends applicants straight to the admissions website.

Timeline: wireframes and copy by 20 December, staging approved by 31 January, launch on Tuesday 9 February 2027 (to be confirmed). Until university status is official, neither website calls ITHS a university.

Your point 5Will this cause duplication?

No. Two websites for one institute are normal when each topic has one home:

  • admission content lives only on the old website;
  • institute content lives only on ithsedu.in;
  • Google is told both sites belong to the same organisation.
