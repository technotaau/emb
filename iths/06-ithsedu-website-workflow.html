How news gets published

Daily activity stays on the campus social accounts. Only institute-level news reaches ithsedu.in: awards, new collaborations, results, published papers, patent applications and programme launches. Target: 2–4 stories a month.

  1. Something happens at a campusAn event, award, visit or result.
  2. The campus coordinator posts it the same dayOn the campus Instagram and Facebook. Daily activity ends here.
  3. Is it important for the whole institute?If yes, send the central team 3–6 real photos, who, what, where and when, and photo consent, on the same day.
  4. The central team writes the story150–400 words, checked against the fact sheet and the claims rules.
  5. The Director's office approvesWithin 24 hours.
  6. Published on ithsedu.in/newsWith a date, the place, and captions on every photo.
  7. Shared on @ithseduAs an Instagram Collab post with the campus account, plus LinkedIn. One post, both profiles, no duplicate.

What we need from you, and the rules

Content checklist (by 18 December)

  • Fact sheet: official name, founding year, campus count, addresses with PIN, phones, emails
  • Mission, vision and 5–8 milestones with years
  • Bios and photos of the Founder, Director and programme heads
  • Faculty list per campus with qualifications and photos
  • For each university: approved collaboration wording, degrees awarded, logo permission
  • Research areas, publications (ORCID), patent application numbers and dates
  • Awards with exact name, issuer and year
  • Real photos of labs, campuses and events, with consent
  • Open job roles; grievance officer contact; logo files

Rules for ithsedu.in

  • No copies of programme, campus, fee or admission pages: link to the admissions website
  • No "No. 1" and no unproven "first" or "only"
  • No placement or salary figures on this site
  • "Patent applications" with numbers, never "patents" unless granted
  • Name the degree-awarding university whenever a programme is mentioned
  • Do not call ITHS a university until it has that status
  • Real photos with consent (a parent's consent under 18); no AI-made people
  • Keep the test site password-protected
