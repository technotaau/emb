## 3. Positioning (TechnoTaau Team recommendation, for approval)

ITHS is **an academic institute that runs translational health-science programmes (clinical embryology, nutrigenetics, stem cell, cancer biology and related fields) in collaboration with partner universities, which award the degrees.**

- ithsedu.in speaks as the institute: mission, research, people, partners, achievements.
- translationalhealthscience.in speaks to applicants and parents: programmes, campuses, eligibility, fees, scholarships, how to apply.
- Every page names the **degree-awarding university and its location** next to the programme (ASCI education guidelines).
- ITHS is **not** described as a university until it holds that status.

## 4. Guardrails (apply to every word on both domains and all social accounts)

Sources: `docs/research/01-industry-research.md` §3.6 and §5 (ASCI, CCPA, UGC), `docs/research/07-seo-aeo-geo-landscape-2026.md` §4.6–4.7 (ASCI, DPDP). Items marked **[VERIFY with counsel]** need the client's lawyer.

1. **No unsubstantiated superlatives or "firsts".** Remove or prove "#1", "India's first", "the only institute in India", "the only institute in the world", "India's premier", "leading", "zero competition in the job market". A "first" claim needs a documented, dated source (ASCI Code; CCPA Guidelines on Misleading Advertisements 2022).
2. **Placement and salary claims:** no "100% placement" (ASCI education guidelines). "95% placement" and salary ranges only with data from the latest completed academic year, the source, and the line "Past record is no guarantee of future prospects" in a font no smaller than the claim. Otherwise say "placement support" with no number.
3. **Patents:** say "patent applications filed" or "patent applications published (No. …)" with the numbers. Never "patents" or "patented" unless grant certificates are verified on IP India. The current "15+ Patents" and "8+ Patents" figures need the application list.
4. **Degree-awarding university named prominently** on every programme, campus, ad and social admission post, with its location (ASCI education guidelines).
5. **The word "university":** ITHS is not called a university, and "UGC recognised" is said only of the partner university, with the exact recognition (for example, "UGC-recognised under Section 2(f)"), checked on ugc.gov.in.
6. **Collaboration wording** follows the signed agreements with each university. UGC does not recognise degrees run under franchise arrangements, so the relationship must be described exactly as the agreements and the university's approvals allow **[VERIFY with counsel]**.
7. **Accreditations** (NAAC, NIRF and similar) are named only for the institution that holds them, with grade and validity. Currently the Shimla page shows "NAAC Accredited (Rayat Bahra University)" on a Bahra University page.
8. **Third-party brand names** (IVF chains, companies, alumni employers) only with written permission or verifiable, current evidence. No logos without permission.
9. **Real photos only** for campus, labs and people. Stock images, if used at all, are never presented as ITHS facilities. AI-generated images or video must be labelled and never show synthetic students, faculty or patients.
10. **Testimonials:** allowed for an academic institute (the NMC rule that bans them applies to medical practice, not ITHS), but only from real, named students with written consent, and parental consent for anyone under 18. No placement or salary claims inside testimonials unless they meet rule 2.
11. **Stem cell content is academic only.** Never promote stem cell therapy or treatments on ITHS channels (NMC advisory of 5 Sep 2026; ICMR guidelines). Course pages describe the science and careers.
12. **Data protection (DPDP Act 2023 and Rules 2025; main duties from about 13–14 May 2027):** consent checkbox and privacy link on every form, data minimisation, verifiable parental consent for applicants under 18, no behavioural tracking or targeted ads at under-18s. Mandatory Aadhaar collection on the admission form is to be reviewed **[VERIFY with counsel]**.
13. **One fact, one value.** Campus count, founding year, programme lists, distances, phone numbers and fees must match across both sites, social bios, Google Business Profiles and aggregators.
14. **Staging sites** are password-protected and `noindex`, and are never linked from the live site.
