# PART A: MESSAGING

## 1. Positioning statement and tagline

### 1.1 Positioning statement (internal: guides every page, never pasted as copy)

> **For** IVF specialists and fertility clinics across India who need clearer information about the endometrium when embryos repeatedly fail to implant,
> **EmbryoGenesis is** a specialised endometrial and reproductive diagnostics lab and academy in Mohali, Punjab,
> **that** offers in-house tests developed in India, reports each test with an honest statement of what it can and cannot tell, and reaches clinics through a growing network of partner collection centres.
> **Unlike** generalist labs and imported test panels, EmbryoGenesis is led by a research scientist, focuses only on reproductive diagnostics and training, and states the evidence status of every test it offers.

The blueprint's one-line strategy is "India's evidence-led endometrial diagnostics partner for IVF clinics". That is the **internal** strategy line. In public copy write **"an evidence-led endometrial diagnostics partner for IVF clinics across India"**. "India's ..." on its own can read as a claim to national standing, which we cannot substantiate.

The "Unlike" clause is for our thinking only. Public copy does not name or compare against competitors, including on turnaround time, unless a dated source has been checked and legal review is complete **[Legal]**.

### 1.2 What we are, and what we are not

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| A diagnostic and research lab | A fertility centre or IVF clinic |
| A partner to the treating doctor | A provider that diagnoses or treats patients directly |
| An academy that trains embryologists | A degree-awarding university (the universities award the degrees) |
| Evidence-honest | A seller of hope, outcomes or "solutions" |

### 1.3 Tagline: "Redefining Fertility Evaluation"

**What it means:** we change how fertility is *evaluated* (tested and understood), not how it is treated. The word "Evaluation" keeps the brand on the diagnostics side of the line, which is also the compliant side.

**Where to use it**
- In the logo lockup (as supplied), in the footer, on social end cards, on the default Open Graph image, in email signatures, on brochures and roll-up banners.
- As a sign-off line under the boilerplate in press releases and decks.

**Rules**
1. Always write it exactly: **Redefining Fertility Evaluation** (title case, no full stop, no ™ unless the client confirms a trademark application for the tagline **[CV]**).
2. Never shorten it to "Redefining Fertility", and never adapt it into "Redefining IVF success", "Redefining Fertility Treatment" or "Redefining Hope". Each of these turns a diagnostics line into a treatment or outcome claim.
3. Do not use it as the page H1 or as a search title. It contains no search terms. H1s describe the page (for example "Endometrial Infection Test for chronic endometritis").
4. Do not set it next to an outcome claim, a baby image or a pregnancy image. Together they imply a promise.
5. In social reels it appears once, on the end card, not as a voice-over slogan in every video.
6. Minimum size: 12 px on screen (the tagline is dropped from the logo lockup below 240 px logo width; see Appendix B).

## 4. Voice and tone

### 4.1 The voice in five words

| Trait | What it means | What it does not mean |
|---|---|---|
| **Scientific** | Uses correct terms, defines them once, cites sources | Showing off, unexplained acronyms |
| **Precise** | Numbers with units and sources; exact names of tests, awards and documents | Vague claims ("advanced", "extensive research") |
| **Calm** | Short sentences, steady pace, no exclamation marks | Cold or clinical towards anxious readers |
| **Evidence-honest** | States limits first-class: "can", "cannot", "the evidence is limited" | Hedging everything until nothing is said |
| **Warm, not salesy** | Acknowledges the reader's situation; invites a conversation with their doctor | Urgency, hype, emotional pressure, emojis in medical copy |

### 4.2 Tone by audience

| Audience | Tone dial |
|---|---|
| Clinicians | Peer-to-peer. Dense but readable. Lead with indication and method. |
| Partner hospitals | Businesslike and clear. Process, responsibilities, timelines. |
| Patients | Gentle and plain. Year 8 reading level where possible. Explain every term. |
| Students | Encouraging and practical. Honest about effort, fees and recognition. |

### 4.3 Writing rules

1. **Answer first.** The first two sentences under every H2 answer the heading's question.
2. **One idea per sentence.** Aim for an average of 15 to 20 words.
3. **Active voice, real subjects.** "Your doctor takes a small sample" not "A sample is taken".
4. **Define once, then abbreviate:** "chronic endometritis (CE)".
5. **Numbers:** digits for 10 and above and for all measurements; "7 to 10 days" in running text, "7–10 days" in tables and UI.
6. **British/Indian English spelling:** centre, programme (but "program" in software contexts), haematology, anaesthesia.
7. **Sentence case** for headings and buttons. Title case only for proper names and the tagline.
8. **No exclamation marks, no emojis** on the website or in medical posts. One emoji maximum in academy social captions.
9. **Name the source** whenever a guideline or study is mentioned: "ESHRE (2023) says ...".
10. **Every medical page ends with** references, the disclaimer and the reviewer line.
11. **Person-first language:** "people with recurrent pregnancy loss", not "RPL patients" in patient copy.
12. **Avoid fear and blame:** never suggest a person caused their loss or failed cycle.

| Context | Don't | Do |
|---|---|---|

### 8.1 Font pairing

## 11. Imagery, illustration and iconography

### 11.1 Photography direction

**Replace all AI-generated stock** (the current site's "ChatGPT-Image-*" files) with real photography of the Mohali lab and team. Real images are a trust signal, a legal safeguard (ASCI: infrastructure visuals must be real) and far lighter once optimised.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Real people, real equipment, real workflow | AI images, stock models in lab coats, glowing DNA, blue holograms |
| Natural light plus soft LED (5000–5600 K), neutral-cool grade | Heavy teal colour wash, lens flares, HDR |
| Hands at work, focused faces, calm spaces | Babies, pregnancy bumps, "happy family" images (they imply an outcome promise) |
| Correct PPE, clean benches, dummy labels | Visible patient names, sample IDs, screens with real data |
| Leave negative space on one side for hero text | Busy backgrounds behind headline areas |

**Shoot logistics:** half-day (about 4 hours) at the Mohali lab; one photographer + one TechnoTaau Team art director; signed model releases from every person shown **[CV]**; no patients photographed; samples are mock or de-identified; lab manager approves each scene for biosafety; RAW capture, delivered as edited high-resolution JPEG plus 3:2 and 4:5 crops.

### 11.3 Science illustration style

- **Style:** flat line illustration, 2 px stroke (scaled to 1.5 px at small sizes), rounded caps and joins, no gradients, no 3D, no glow.
- **Colour:** lines in Deep Teal; fills in Mist, Green Tint and Gold Tint; highlights in Teal or Green; Gold only for one emphasis element per diagram.
- **Labels:** live HTML text where possible, otherwise Inter 14 px+ inside the SVG. Every label must pass contrast against its fill.
- **Accuracy:** every diagram (endometrium layers, cycle timing, biopsy window, sample journey) is reviewed by Prof. Purkait before use, like text.
- **Subjects to avoid:** foetuses, babies, sperm-racing clichés, anything suggesting sex or gender.
- **Format:** SVG, optimised (SVGO), under 30 KB each, with `<title>` and `<desc>`.
- Starter set needed for launch: endometrium layers; menstrual cycle with biopsy window; sample journey; chronic endometritis (plasma cells, simplified); how a report is read; embryology career path.

### 11.4 Icon set

- **Recommended:** **Lucide** (ISC licence, free), outline style, 24 px grid, stroke 1.75 px, rounded. It includes microscope, flask, test-tube, DNA, file-text, map-pin, truck, calendar, shield-check, graduation-cap, users and book-open.
- **Custom additions (6, drawn to match Lucide):** endometrium, biopsy pipette, slide, cold-chain box, sample kit, collection centre.
- **Delivery:** inline SVG or one SVG sprite; `currentColor` so icons inherit Deep Teal/Teal Mid. Remove Font Awesome 4 and 5, icofont, ekiticons and eicons.
- **Sizes:** 20 px (inline with text), 24 px (UI), 32 px (cards), 48 px (feature blocks). Meaningful icons need 3:1 contrast (Teal Mid or Deep Teal on light; Teal on White only).

## Appendix A: Contrast script

Run with `python3 wcag_contrast.py`. It prints the tables in section 7.2.

```python
"""WCAG 2.2 contrast checker for the EmbryoGenesis palette (TechnoTaau Team)."""
def lum(h):
    h = h.lstrip('#'); r, g, b = (int(h[i:i+2], 16) / 255 for i in (0, 2, 4))
    f = lambda c: c / 12.92 if c <= 0.04045 else ((c + 0.055) / 1.055) ** 2.4
    return 0.2126 * f(r) + 0.7152 * f(g) + 0.0722 * f(b)

def ratio(a, b):
    hi, lo = sorted((lum(a), lum(b)), reverse=True); return (hi + 0.05) / (lo + 0.05)

def grade(r):
    return "AAA" if r >= 7 else "AA" if r >= 4.5 else "AA-large/UI" if r >= 3 else "fail"

BG = {"White": "#FFFFFF", "Paper": "#F7FAFA", "Ink": "#0F2426", "Deep Teal": "#0E6970"}
FG = {"Teal": "#25A2AA", "Teal Mid": "#11858E", "Deep Teal": "#0E6970", "Green": "#218A6A",
      "Gold": "#C39F4E", "Teal Dark": "#0B5C62", "Green Dark": "#1A6E55", "Gold Dark": "#7A5C1B",
      "Gold Light": "#E6C77F", "Ink": "#0F2426", "Body": "#2B3F42", "Muted": "#56696B",
      "Muted on dark": "#9FB3B5", "Border Strong": "#748C8E", "Border": "#D3E0E1",
      "Error": "#B42318", "White": "#FFFFFF"}

for n, c in FG.items():
    print(n, " | ".join("n/a" if c == b else f"{ratio(c, b):.2f} ({grade(ratio(c, b))})"
                        for b in BG.values()))
```
