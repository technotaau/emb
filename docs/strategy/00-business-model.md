## 1. One-line summary

EmbryoGenesis is the **diagnostic and clinical arm of a group founded by Prof. (Dr.) Pulakes Purkait**. It sells in-house endometrial tests (and related immune/regenerative therapies) to couples facing IVF failure or repeated miscarriage, mostly **through IVF clinics and gynaecologists who take the biopsy**. Its university-campus lab is linked to a sister academic institute (ITHS) that runs embryology degrees.

## 4. How a patient actually reaches a test (our understanding)

```
Couple has IVF failure / repeated miscarriage
        │
        ├─► (route 1) Their IVF doctor suggests ERFA ──► biopsy at the clinic (day 21–23 / LH+7 / P+6)
        │                                                      │
        └─► (route 2) Couple finds EmbryoGenesis online ──► calls "Book Now" ──► sent to a partner clinic
                                                                 or to Mohali for the biopsy
                                                                       │
                                     sample reaches the Mohali lab ◄───┘
                                                │
                                   report in 7–10 days ──► doctor (and couple)
                                                │
                              therapy suggested (LIT-H2 / PBMC / PRP …) [confirm where it is given]
```

Both routes need a doctor for the biopsy. That is why IVF clinics are the real gatekeepers, even when the couple pays.

## 5. What this means for the website (with the old content kept)

The client has decided to keep the old site's text and change only the design. The business model tells us **what to put first and how to route each visitor**:

1. **Couples are the largest visitor group** (the YouTube views, the "Book Now" buttons and the blog are all couple-facing). The homepage hero, test cards and "Book Now" keep speaking to them, as the old site does.
2. **Doctors decide.** Without new pages, the redesign still shows the proof doctors look for, using content the client already has: the scientific team, Dr. Wadhwa's MBBS MD, the patents, MoUs, and the collection-centre list. These move higher on the homepage.
3. **Students are a second, separate funnel.** The admissions bar stays at the top, and Courses keep their own forms. Because ITHS already runs a full admissions website, embryogenesis.in should link its "Apply Now" to ITHS or share the same admissions team, so leads do not split [confirm].
4. **Every form must say which funnel it feeds** (test booking, therapy enquiry, admission, training) so the right person calls back.
5. **The test-to-therapy loop** is the core story: diagnose (ERFA/EIT/Endo-Immune) → personalised plan. The homepage tabs show Diagnostic first, Therapeutic second, Academic third, matching the order of the business.

| Revenue mix | **Diagnostic tests and therapies earn about equally**; academy is separate | Homepage shows Diagnostic and Therapeutic services side by side, both open (no hidden tab). Academy gets its own section lower down |
