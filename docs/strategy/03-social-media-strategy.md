### Pillar 1: Clinician Science Briefs
- **What:** Short, cited explanations of the science behind endometrial testing. Lead with **chronic endometritis** (ESHRE 2023 RIF: assessment "can be considered"). Present ERFA and Endo-Immune as specialised, clinician-directed tools with an honest evidence-status line.
- **Examples:** "ESHRE 2023 on RIF: what 'can be considered' means for CE testing" (document carousel); "Biopsy timing basics: natural vs HRT cycles" (carousel, from the clinician hub); "CD138 explained" (reel 4); "How we report: method, criteria, limitations" (carousel).
- **Rules:** cite source and year on the slide; add "Information for healthcare professionals"; no outcome or success-rate claims.

### Pillar 2: Network & Partnerships
- **What:** How the collection network works, who the partners are (with their written permission), partner onboarding, MoUs.
- **Examples:** "Our collection centres: Navi Mumbai, Udaipur, Jalandhar" (map post); "How a sample travels to our lab" (reel 5); "What it takes to become a collection point" (carousel); "MoU with Guru Ghasidas Vishwavidyalaya, Bilaspur (Nov 2024)" (throwback post).
- **Rules:** partner names and logos only with written consent; no implied endorsement of results; no patient data.

### Pillar 3: Inside the Lab
- **What:** Real footage and photos of the lab, sample receipt, QC steps, people at work.
- **Examples:** "From sample receipt to report: 6 quality checkpoints" (carousel); "Meet our Quality Manager, Dr. Vidhu Wadhwa" (photo + short quote, with consent; add registration number if she is a registered medical practitioner); "What our lab trainees learn in week one" (reel).
- **Rules:** **never AI-generated**; staff consent forms on file; no patient names, faces, sample labels or screens visible.

### Pillar 4: Academy & Careers
- **What:** Career education for students; academy facts; the "trained embryologists for clinics" B2B story.
- **Examples:** "B.Sc vs M.Sc Clinical Embryology" (reel 7); "What does an embryologist do?" (reel 6); "ART Rules and embryologist staffing" (reel 11); "Admissions open for [course]: awarding university, duration, eligibility" (carousel).
- **Rules (ASCI education guidelines):** name the **degree-awarding university and its location** prominently on every academy post; no "100% placement" or "assured placement"; any placement or salary figure only from the latest completed academic year with evidence, plus "past record is no guarantee of future prospects"; infrastructure visuals must be real.

### Pillar 5: Myth vs Evidence
- **What:** One common belief, the evidence, and what the evidence does **not** say. Always closes with "talk to your doctor".
- **Examples:** "One failed IVF cycle means you can't conceive" (reel 9); "More tests after IVF failure are always better" (reel 12); "A lab report tells you what to do next" (reel 10 angle).
- **Rules:** empathetic tone; no babies, pregnancy tests or bumps as imagery; no promises; cite the source in the caption and on the references page.

## 4. AI Informative Reels system

### 4.1 Format specifications

| Spec | Standard |
|---|---|
| Aspect / resolution | 9:16, 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, H.264 MP4 |
| Length | 45–60 s target (hard range 30–75 s). Instagram recommends reels to non-followers up to 3 minutes; shorter, complete-able reels hold watch time better |
| Hook | Spoken and on-screen within the **first 2 seconds**. A question or a surprising, accurate statement. No logo intro |
| Captions | Burned-in, sentence-by-sentence, 2 lines max, brand font, white on a Deep Teal (#0E6970) box or with a dark shadow. Minimum about 48 px. Keep text inside the safe zone (about 250 px clear at top, 420 px at bottom, 60 px at sides) |
| On-screen beats | One key phrase per 4–6 seconds, matching the voiceover |
| Voice | Calm, clear Indian English, about 140 words per minute. Either a real staff voice or a **stock** AI voice (never a clone of a real person) |
| Music | Low (about −20 dB under voice), royalty-free or the platform's commercial library. Business accounts must not use trending copyrighted tracks |
| AI label | On-screen text "AI-generated visuals" (and "+ AI voice" if used) in the top-left for the **first 3 seconds and on the end card**, plus the platform toggle (4.5) |
| Reviewer line | End card: "Script reviewed by Prof. (Dr.) Pulakes Purkait, PhD · [DD Mon YYYY]" |
| Disclaimer | End card: "Information only. Not medical advice." |
| End card (2.5 s) | Deep Teal background, logo, "Redefining Fertility Evaluation", embryogenesis.in, reviewer line, AI label, disclaimer. Gold only as a thin divider line |
| Cover frame | Designed cover: title in 4–6 words, consistent series badge ("Science in 60s", "Lab Term", "Myth vs Evidence", "Inside the Lab") so the grid reads as a library |
| Captions (post text) | First line repeats the hook; 3–5 lines of value; reference line; AI disclosure line; **max 5 hashtags** (Instagram now caps posts and reels at five) |

### 4.2 The key rule: what AI may and may not be used for

The NMC guidelines (6 Oct 2026) state that an "AI generated promotional campaign for furtherance of commercial interest is prohibited", that AI may not create or alter a patient's image, testimonial, voice or clinical outcome, or a synthetic endorsement, and that compliant AI content "shall compulsorily carry the source mark". Reports also say AI content is treated as advertising when its content or manner of dissemination has a "promotional character". Whether these guidelines bind a diagnostic lab directly is not stated in the coverage we found, so we apply them in full.

| Content type | AI visuals / AI voice allowed? | Production route |
|---|---|---|
| General science education (endometrium, implantation, CE, lab terms, myths) | **Yes**, labelled | Templates A, B, C |
| Career education (what an embryologist does, B.Sc vs M.Sc, ART Rules) | Yes for motion graphics; **no AI people** | Templates A, B |
| Anything about **our services, tests by brand name, turnaround, network, lab, team or courses** | **No generative AI.** Real footage, real voice, conventional motion graphics | Template D |
| Ads (any paid promotion) | **No** generative AI visuals or voice | Real photo/video + design |
| Therapies | Not produced at all (legal hold) | n/a |

Using AI for **productivity** (drafting a script outline, auto-captions, noise reduction, translation of our own subtitles) is not "synthetic media" in this sense, but the script is always rewritten and fact-checked by a person and reviewed by Prof. Purkait.

### 4.4 Production workflow (one reel)
