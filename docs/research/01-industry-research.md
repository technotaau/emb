## 0. Executive summary

1. **The market is real and growing, but figures vary a lot between sources.** The Indian IVF services market is roughly **USD 0.9 to 1.0 billion (2024-25)**. Analyst CAGR estimates run from **8% to 16%**. Commonly cited demand figures are about **27.5 million infertile couples**, around **2-2.5 lakh IVF cycles a year**, and a projected **~4 lakh cycles by 2030**. Growth is moving to tier-2 and tier-3 cities, where treatment costs 25-30% less, and Punjab cities such as Amritsar are named in that trend. Large chains (Indira, Nova, Birla, Oasis) are expanding hub-and-spoke networks. That creates **B2B referral demand for specialist diagnostics**.
2. **The scientific evidence is weak for almost everything EmbryoGenesis sells.** ESHRE (2023 add-ons good practice recommendations) says **"not recommended"** for all of the following: commercial endometrial receptivity tests, peripheral and uterine NK/immune testing, immunomodulating treatments including **PBMC**, intraovarian and intrauterine **PRP**, and **stem cell therapy** for POI, poor ovarian reserve or thin endometrium. ESHRE's RPL guideline says **LIT "should not be used"** (strong recommendation). The UK HFEA rates endometrial receptivity testing **red**, PRP **red**, and IVIG and steroids **red**. Chronic endometritis testing and antibiotics are the only exception: ESHRE's RIF recommendations say they **"can be considered"**. Content that claims these tests or therapies "improve success" will not hold up against these sources, will damage trust with the IVF doctors who refer patients, and creates regulatory exposure.
3. **Compliance red flags on the current site, by severity:**
   - **CRITICAL: Stem cell therapy.** On 5 Sept 2026 NMC issued an advisory following the Supreme Court's 30 Jan 2026 judgment in *Yash Charitable Trust v. UoI*. It says stem cell therapy may be offered as standard care **only for approved (haematological/oncological) indications**, and that *"any unauthorized administration, prescription, promotion or advertisement thereof, beyond such approved indications, shall constitute professional misconduct."* ICMR's 2017 guidelines already treated all other stem cell use as investigational and allowed it only inside approved trials. Google Ads also bans ads for unproven stem cell therapy. **Remove "Stem Cell Therapy" as a service.** At most, describe it as research, with a CTRI-registered trial and ethics approval.
   - **HIGH: "Patented."** The founder's own profile lists ERFA as *Patent No. 202431001616 **A***, and every other entry is likewise an "A" (published application) number or "applied". None is shown as granted. Section 120 of the Patents Act penalises falsely representing that something is patented. Indian courts also read Section 3(i) as excluding diagnostic methods, including in vitro ones, from patentability, so grant is not assured. Use **"patent application filed / published (App. No. …)"** until a grant certificate exists.
   - **HIGH: "As the best fertility centre"** and **"India's trusted…"**. These are unsubstantiated superlatives under the ASCI Code and the CCPA Misleading Ads Guidelines 2022. Clause 6.1 of the IMC 2002 ethics regulations also prohibits self-aggrandising solicitation, and the 2002 code is still in force because the 2023 NMC conduct rules are in abeyance. The site is also not a fertility *centre* in the ART Act sense, since it does not offer IVF.
   - **HIGH: LIT, PBMC, PRP and molecular hydrogen** are presented as therapies with no disclaimers. Under ESHRE and HFEA they are not recommended, or rated red. PBMC and LIT involve processing cells, which may bring them under CDSCO's "cell-derived product" definition in the NDCT Rules **[VERIFY with regulatory counsel]**. Any treatment must be delivered by an NMC/SMC-registered doctor, and the site does not currently name the treating doctors.
   - **HIGH: Infertility ("sterility in women") and "impotence" are on the schedule of the Drugs & Magic Remedies Act, 1954.** Any ad for a *drug or remedy* that claims to cure or treat them is prohibited. Diagnostic tests and procedures sit in a grey zone, so avoid cure language entirely.
   - **MEDIUM: DPDP Act and Rules.** The Rules were notified 13 Nov 2025 and core obligations apply from **13 May 2027**. A January 2026 proposal would move the deadline to 13 Nov 2026 **[UNVERIFIED; not notified as of the latest information found]**. Health data, and **minors in student-admission forms**, call for consent-first forms. Section 9 bans tracking and targeted ads aimed at children.
   - **MEDIUM: Education claims.** ASCI's education guidelines restrict 100% placement claims, require substantiated placement and salary claims, and require the degree-awarding institution to be named prominently. UGC does not recognise degrees run under **franchise arrangements**. The "ITHS + Bahra University" structure (Bahra University is in Himachal Pradesh, while ITHS has campuses in Mohali, Shimla and Chhattisgarh) needs a clear, documented legal basis **[VERIFY]**.
4. **Digital behaviour.** India has ~886 million internet users and 98% of them consume Indic-language content. Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode are live in Hindi and 7 other Indian languages. AI Overviews appear on an estimated 44-88% of health queries and cut organic CTR sharply. YouTube health "source information panels" are now open to registered Indian health professionals. In this setting, trust signals (named and credentialed experts, citations, accreditation, transparent pricing) and WhatsApp-first conversion matter more than ranking alone.
5. **Positioning recommendation.** Present EmbryoGenesis as an **evidence-honest endometrial and reproductive-immunology diagnostics and research lab plus an embryology training academy**, not a "fertility centre". Sell to **IVF clinicians (B2B)** and to **students**, and give patients careful, cited education. The strongest assets that hold up to scrutiny are the chronic endometritis testing ("can be considered" under ESHRE RIF), research and publication output, training, and the B2B sample-collection network (Navi Mumbai, Udaipur, Jalandhar).

## 2. Reproductive immunology and endometrial testing: trends and evidence status

### 2.1 Global test landscape

- **Transcriptomic receptivity tests:** ERA (Igenomix, 248 genes), ERPeak (48 genes, RT-qPCR), rsERT (175 genes, RNA-seq), Win-Test, beREADY, ERMap and others ([2023 SWOT review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10472299); [ERPeak](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8967283); [rsERT](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/medicine/articles/10.3389/fmed.2024.1369317/pdf)).
- **Microbiome and infection:** EMMA (endometrial microbiome) and ALICE (chronic endometritis pathogens) from Igenomix. CD138/MUM1 immunohistochemistry for plasma cells in chronic endometritis.
- **ReceptivaDx:** BCL6 marker for occult endometriosis. I did not research it in depth **[GAP]**.
- **Immune testing:** peripheral NK %, uNK counts, KIR/HLA-C genotyping, Th1/Th2 and cytokine panels.
- **Trend:** the field is expanding commercially, especially in Asia, while evidence bodies have become *more* sceptical since 2022 after the large US RCT (Doyle et al., 2022; 767 women with euploid blastocysts). That trial found receptivity-timed transfer gave **no improvement** in live birth (58.5% vs 61.9%; RR 0.95) (cited within the [ESHRE 2023 add-ons paper](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/10178220/1/dead184.pdf)). Pooled meta-analyses find no live-birth benefit in unselected FET patients and possible benefit only in selected RIF patients ([2023 meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36414088/); [2022 meta-analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9092494)).

**ESHRE's wording scale (Table 1 of the add-ons paper):** "Not recommended" means *"Based on safety concerns and/or lack of efficacy and/or lack of biological rationale, the test/intervention should not be applied to patients. Further evaluation … only in strict research settings."* ESHRE also states that 95% of its 42 recommendations rest on low-quality evidence. So the honest framing is **"emerging / investigational / evidence is limited and evolving,"** not "proven".

**HFEA:** no add-on is currently rated green. The page was published 16 Oct 2023 and last reviewed 28 Sep 2026.

### 2.3 What this means for content

- Write **evidence-graded pages**. Each test or therapy page should have a "What the evidence says" box that cites ESHRE, HFEA and Cochrane by name, and a "Who might consider it" section that limits use to RIF, RPL or thin endometrium cases after clinician discussion. Done well, this becomes a *differentiator*: AI search engines and clinicians favour balanced sources, and competitors mostly overclaim.
- Never say "cure", "guaranteed pregnancy", "increase success by X%", or "uNK cells attack the embryo".
- Publish EmbryoGenesis's own validation data (sample sizes, methods, peer-reviewed papers). If there is none, say that studies are ongoing. Register trials on **CTRI** and link them.
- The founder's profile lists 30 international articles. Link those relevant to ERFA, LIT or receptivity directly, as that supports E-E-A-T.

## 3. India regulatory and advertising compliance

### 3.1 ART (Regulation) Act 2021 and Rules 2022 (amended 2026)

- **Scope:** ART clinics (Level 1: IUI; Level 2: IVF and others) and ART banks must register. **[VERIFY]** whether EmbryoGenesis's activities (semen processing, sperm/oocyte-related lab work, any intrauterine procedures) bring it under ART clinic or bank registration. Diagnostics alone probably do not, but intrauterine PBMC or PRP done in conjunction with IVF cycles should be assessed by counsel.
- **Section 32:** bans sex-selective ART and **any advertisement of it, including on the internet**. Penalty: 5-10 years jail and/or ₹10-25 lakh fine ([advocatekhoj s.32](https://www.advocatekhoj.com/library/bareacts/assistedreproductive2021/32.php)). Content must never reference gender or sex selection, including Hindi or Punjabi search terms like "beta paane ke upay".
- **Schedule I, Part 1 of the ART Rules 2022** sets staff requirements. Each **Level 2 clinic needs 2 embryologists (one senior, one junior)**. A **senior embryologist** needs a PG in clinical embryology (on site) plus 1 year of lab experience, *or* an MBBS or life-sciences PG plus 1 year of certified onsite embryology training plus 2 years in a Level 2 clinic lab. A **junior embryologist** needs a life-science graduate degree plus 3 years' experience, or a PG ([ART Rules 2022](https://www.thc.nic.in/Central%20Governmental%20Rules/Assisted%20Reproductive%20Technology%20(Regulation)%20Rules,%202022,%20%20.pdf)). This is a **regulatory demand driver for the education business** and should be cited on course pages.
- Confidentiality and record-keeping duties under the Act overlap with DPDP.

### 3.2 PCPNDT Act 1994

- Bans pre-conception and pre-natal sex selection and determination, and **advertising of sex determination in any form, including internet** (Section 22). Genetic labs and clinics doing pre-natal diagnostics must register. Google also lists pre-natal gender determination as "Not allowed" in India ([Google Ads policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/176031)).
- **Website risk:** negative-keyword lists, blog moderation and comment moderation must screen out sex-selection queries.

### 3.3 Stem cell therapy (CRITICAL)

- **ICMR National Guidelines for Stem Cell Research 2017:** any stem cell use in patients, other than haematopoietic stem cell reconstitution for approved indications, *"is investigational at present … must only be done within the purview of an approved and monitored clinical trial … and not for offering it as therapy"* ([Mondaq summary](https://www.mondaq.com/Article/652768); [full text](https://web.iitd.ac.in/~elangovan/ethics/doc/National_Guidelines_StemCellResearch-2017_0.pdf)).
- **NDCT Rules 2019 (amended 2022):** a "cell or stem cell derived product" is a **new drug** that needs CDSCO approval if more than minimally manipulated ([CDSCO](https://cdsco.gov.in/opencms/opencms/en/Acts-and-rules/New-Drugs/); [PMDA/CDSCO slides](https://www.pmda.go.jp/files/000269718.pdf)).
- **Supreme Court, 30 Jan 2026** (*Yash Charitable Trust v. UoI*, W.P.(C) 369/2022, 2026 INSC 96): stem cell therapy for ASD cannot be offered as routine commercial treatment outside clinical trials.
- **NMC advisory, 25 Mar 2026:** routine stem cell use is limited to MoHFW's list of 32 approved conditions, all haematological or oncological ([IndiaMedToday](https://indiamedtoday.com/nmc-tightens-stem-cell-therapy-rules-limits-use-to-32-approved-diseases-after-supreme-court-ruling/)).
- **NMC EMRB advisory, 5 Sep 2026** (primary text reviewed: [PDF](https://medicaldialogues.in/pdf_upload/2026/09/07/nmc-advisory-05092026-474464.pdf)): *"Stem Cell Therapy may be offered as standard clinical care, ONLY for approved indications as listed in ICMR/DHR/MoHFW guidelines. Any unauthorized administration, prescription, promotion or advertisement thereof, beyond such approved indications, shall constitute professional misconduct."* It cites IMC Regulations 2002, clauses 1.9 (evasion of legal restrictions, including the DMR Act) and 7.22 (violation of ICMR guidelines is misconduct).
- **Google Ads:** "Promotion of speculative and/or experimental medical treatments" is prohibited, which covers most stem cell, cellular and gene therapies ([policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/176031); [2019 announcement](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/google-bans-ads-unproven-medical-treatments-n1050811)).
- **Action:** remove stem cell therapy from service menus, ads, schema and social posts. If research exists, create a "Research" page with the ethics committee, CTRI number and an investigational disclaimer, and no booking CTA.

### 3.4 Medical ethics and advertising (NMC / IMC 2002)

- The NMC RMP (Professional Conduct) Regulations 2023 were **put in abeyance on 23 Aug 2023**. The **IMC (Professional Conduct, Etiquette and Ethics) Regulations 2002 remain operative** ([Medical Dialogues](https://medicaldialogues.in/health-news/nmc/breaking-news-nmc-puts-its-controversial-registered-medical-practitioner-professional-conduct-regulations-2023-put-on-hold-116314); [BW Healthcare](https://www.bwhealthcareworld.com/article/nmc-puts-new-regulations-on-docs-in-abeyance-directs-to-follow-old-guidelines-from-2002-488890)). I found no change to this status up to Oct 2026.
- **Clause 6.1** prohibits soliciting patients directly or indirectly, *by a physician, group of physicians or by institutions or organisations*. Self-aggrandising ads, unwarranted claims and patient testimonials used as solicitation are risky. Permitted: factual announcements (name, qualifications, registration number, services, timings, fees).
- **Practical rules:** list every treating doctor with **NMC/SMC registration number**. Do not use paid or fake reviews. Get informed consent before using any patient story. Do not use "Dr." for non-medical PhDs in a way that implies medical practice; write "Prof. (Dr.) Pulakes Purkait, PhD (Reproductive/Genetic Sciences) – Scientific Director". The founder's listed degrees are an M.Sc. (Vidyasagar University), a PhD (Andhra University) and a postdoc (Panjab University), not an MBBS ([profile](https://embryogenesis.in/pulakes-purkait-profile/)). **Therapies must be shown as delivered by named registered medical practitioners.**

### 3.5 Drugs & Magic Remedies (Objectionable Advertisements) Act 1954

- Prohibits ads for drugs or remedies claiming to diagnose, cure, mitigate, treat or prevent scheduled conditions. The Schedule includes **"sterility in women"** and **"sexual impotence"**. Section 3 also covers sexual capacity, miscarriage and menstrual disorders ([TN govt copy](https://tnslaim.tn.gov.in/dmr_rules.html); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drugs_and_Magic_Remedies_(Objectionable_Advertisements)_Act,_1954)).
- **Scope caveat:** the Act targets *drugs and magic remedies*. Diagnostic services and procedures are not clearly covered, but molecular hydrogen "therapy kits" (the founder's profile lists an "LIT-H2 Antioxidant Kit" design registration), supplements or any product sold for infertility would be. **Avoid any "treats infertility" claim for products.**

### 3.6 ASCI Code and CCPA guidelines (claims)

- **ASCI:** claims must be truthful and substantiated. Superlatives like "best" or "No. 1" need objective evidence. Healthcare is the top category for upheld complaints: 82 of 200 upheld in one report, including a **fertility centre's "High Success Rate" claim found misleading** ([ASCI report](https://www.ascionline.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/asci-oct-ccc.pdf)). Awards and rankings must come from credible independent bodies and name the source and date ([ASCI awards guideline](https://www.ascionline.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/asci-guidelines-for-usage-of-awards_rankings-in-advertisements.pdf)). The "Best Innovator Awardee: NBHC 2024 at IIT Delhi" award can be used **with its exact name, issuer and year**, and it must not be implied to cover the whole centre.
- **CCPA Guidelines on Misleading Advertisements 2022** (Consumer Protection Act 2019): claims must be substantiable, and research cited must give source and date. Disclaimers cannot cure a misleading claim. Penalties go up to ₹10 lakh, or ₹50 lakh for repeat offences ([Nishith Desai](https://www.nishithdesai.com/research-and-articles/hotline/technology-law-analysis/guidelines-on-misleading-advertisements-and-endorsements-notified-under-the-consumer-protection-act-2019-6172); [IASbaba](https://iasbaba.com/2022/07/should-endorsers-be-held-responsible-for-claims-in-advertising/)). **Influencer endorsements** must be disclosed, and health endorsers should be qualified.
- **ASCI education guidelines** ([PDF](https://www.ascionline.in/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/ASCI-GUIDELINES-FOR-EDUCATIONAL-INSTITUTIONS-PROGRAMMES-AND-PLATFORMS.pdf)):
  - No "100% placement" claims.
  - Placement, salary, topper and ranking claims only from the **latest completed academic year** and with evidence.
  - The disclaimer "past record is no guarantee of future prospects" must be shown in a font no smaller than the claim.
  - Infrastructure visuals must be real.
  - **The name and location of the degree-awarding or affiliated institution must be displayed prominently.**

### 3.7 "Patented" claim (HIGH)

- The founder profile lists 14 IP items. ERFA is *202431001616 A*, ReceptivityPX *202411059621 A*, and the LIT plus H2 method *202411037742 A*. The others are "A" applications or "applied", plus design registrations. **None is stated as granted** ([profile](https://embryogenesis.in/pulakes-purkait-profile/)). A suffix "A" in Indian numbering normally denotes a published application.
- **Patents Act s.120:** falsely representing that an article is patented, or is the subject of an application, is punishable. The original penalty was a fine up to ₹1 lakh. The Jan Vishwas amendment reportedly converted this to a **penalty up to ₹10 lakh plus ₹1,000 per day** while the claim continues ([IP India s.120](https://www.ipindia.gov.in/acts/patent-act-1970/section-120); [ICSI 2024](https://www.icsi.edu/media/webmodules/Academics/IPRL&P_PPOS_SUPP_JUNE08032024.pdf)) **[VERIFY current text]**.
- **Section 3(i)** excludes "any process for the … diagnostic, therapeutic … treatment of human beings". The Madras HC (CUHK appeals) read "diagnostic" to cover **in vitro** methods too ([Intepat](https://www.intepat.com/blog/patenting-medical-procedures-india-section-3i); [FICPI](https://ficpi.org/blog/understanding-patentability-diagnostic-methods-indian-patent-law)). Method claims for ERFA, ReceptivityPX and LIT may therefore face objections. Kits and devices fall outside 3(i).
- **Action:** replace "Patented diagnostic test" with "Indian patent application published (No. 202431001616 A)". Remove the six un-captioned certificate images or caption each one accurately (application vs grant vs design registration). Verify each status on IP India InPASS.

### 3.8 DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025

- The Rules were notified **13 Nov 2025 (G.S.R. 846(E))**. The Board operates immediately, Consent Manager provisions apply at 12 months (~Nov 2026), and **core obligations (notice, consent, security, breach reporting, rights) apply from 13 May 2027** ([PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2190014&reg=3&lang=2); [dcomply](https://dpdpa.dcomply.in/rules/)). MeitY reportedly proposed (Jan 2026) shortening this to 12 months, i.e. 13 Nov 2026, starting with Significant Data Fiduciaries ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/meity-may-cut-compliance-timeline-for-key-dpdp-rules-to-12-months-126012201293_1.html)). I found **no notification** of that change **[VERIFY]**.
- **Requirements for this site** (from the Act itself; statute text not re-fetched in this research):
  - Consent must be free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous, with a clear affirmative action. Pre-ticked boxes are not valid.
  - The notice must be itemised and can be given in English or any Eighth Schedule language. **Hindi and Punjabi notices are advisable.**
  - Consent must be as easy to withdraw as to give.
  - **Children (under 18):** verifiable parental consent is required, and **tracking, behavioural monitoring and targeted advertising directed at children are prohibited (s.9)**. Many B.Sc applicants are 17. Admission forms must ask for date of birth or age and route minors to a parental-consent flow, and the site must not retarget them.
  - Security safeguards and breach notification to the Board and affected people are required. Maximum penalties reach ₹250 crore (security) and ₹200 crore (children).
- **Health-specific:** the DPDP Act has no "sensitive data" class, but fertility data is high-harm. Practical steps:
  - Avoid sending form contents to Meta or Google pixels.
  - Use server-side tagging with PII stripped.
  - Keep consent logs.
  - Restrict WhatsApp CRM access.
  - Keep a data retention schedule.
  - Use a separate consent for any marketing.

### 6.3 Things NOT to do

- Do not market stem cell therapy as a service in any form.
- Do not use "best", "No. 1", "India's first" or "trusted by thousands" without proof.
- Do not say "patented" until a grant exists.
- Do not publish success-rate or pregnancy-rate claims from internal unpublished data.
- Do not make cure or guarantee claims, or claims that uNK or immunity is "rejecting your baby".
- Do not post before-and-after or baby-photo testimonials without consent and context.
- Do not use any gender or sex-selection terms.
- Do not run remarketing to health audiences on Google, or to minors anywhere.
- Do not send form data to ad pixels.
