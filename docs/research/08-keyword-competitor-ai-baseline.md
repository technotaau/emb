## 1. Summary: the findings that matter for decisions

1. **The demand is there, but the brand is not.** People in India search heavily, in English and Hinglish, for IVF failure, ERA, chronic endometritis, thin endometrium, recurrent miscarriage and embryology careers. EmbryoGenesis appeared in **none** of the non-branded results checked.
   - Brand searches find nothing about it. "EmbryoGenesis Mohali" returns unrelated Mohali IVF directories, and "Pulakes Purkait" returns West Bengal politicians.
   - The site is in the index: a search restricted to embryogenesis.in returns `/diagnostic/` and `/unexplained-infertility/`. The indexed `/diagnostic/` title is still the old **"Best Infertility Diagnostics in India"**, a superlative the new build already removes.
2. **Who owns the answers today:**
   - **Shree IVF / ImmuRepro (Mumbai)** is the clinic source that surfaced most often across the clinical clusters (ERA/EMMA/ALICE, NK/BCL-6, chronic endometritis, thin endometrium, RIF, ERA cost).
   - **Medical literature** (PMC, ESHRE, University of Melbourne IVF evidence pages, NBC Washington) dominates "is it worth it" questions.
   - **Apollo, Birla, Indira and Nova** dominate "what is ERA" and its cost.
   - **Careers360, Shiksha and Zollege** dominate every course, eligibility, fee and salary query.
3. **Five areas nobody owns, where EmbryoGenesis can become the cited source within the guardrails:**
   - **(a) "ERA vs ERFA" and IHC-based receptivity testing.** No third-party source mentions ERFA. The proxy search reads "ERFA" as a typo for ERA, and its summary of embryogenesis.in said there is "no independent evaluation of the ERFA test itself".
   - **(b) Endometrial biopsy explained for patients:** timing, pain and pipelle.
   - **(c) Hinglish and Hindi questions.** Hindi queries return English foreign pages.
   - **(d) Endometrial TB-PCR / genital TB and infertility.** Only Indian journal papers rank.
   - **(e) North-India embryology admissions.** Autocomplete returns 8 or more "Rayat Bahra University … clinical embryology (fees)" variants.
4. **New evidence on CD138 testing needs a reviewer decision.**
   - A 2026 *Human Reproduction* study (Odendaal et al.) concludes that "CD138-based CE testing and treatment should not be performed outside of a research context" ([White Rose eprint](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/239619)).
   - *Fertility and Sterility* 2022 (Ryan et al.) found plasma cells in 59.3% of follicular-phase biopsies against 19.7% of luteal-phase biopsies ([Stanford record](https://stanfordhealthcare.org/publications/858/858828.html)).
   - ESHRE 2023 still says chronic endometritis assessment "can be considered" ([PMC10270320](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10270320/)).
   - The brief's plan to "lead with EIT" therefore needs Prof. Purkait and Dr. Wadhwa to decide how EIT is framed and when its biopsy is taken.
5. **Two current pages are risky for AI answers:**
   - ESHRE 2023 says "uterine NK cell testing is not recommended" (same source). This affects how the Endo-Immune Test and the EIP post are framed.
   - The only EmbryoGenesis pages an AI tool could cite for recurrent miscarriage or IVF failure in Hindi are the **LIT post** and the **Hindi post**. The Hindi post says LIT "increases the chance of successful conception" and promotes PRP and stem cell therapy. This conflicts with the ESHRE 2022 RPL guideline (report 02) and guardrail 4. Content stays as the client decided, but this is the biggest reputational exposure in AI answers.
6. **Keyword traps.** In Indian autocomplete:
   - "ICSI course/training" means the *Institute of Company Secretaries of India*.
   - "RIF test" means rifampicin resistance (TB).
   - "ERA cost" returns a crypto token in generic search.
   - "soil test" means agriculture.
   - "PBMC therapy" means physical therapy (report 02).
   - "embryogenesis" is the biology term ([Wikidata Q28962](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q28962)).

   Always use the full phrase, for example "ICSI training for embryologists", "recurrent implantation failure" and "ERFA endometrial test".
7. **Do not chase cost keywords.** Apollo publishes an ERA cost FAQ (₹35,000–40,000) and Birla runs 39 city "ERA test cost" pages (₹39,000–41,000). The no-price rule makes these unwinnable. Answer "what affects the cost" in an FAQ without figures, and spend the effort on explainer depth instead.
8. **The entity footprint is thin and inconsistent:**
   - A Google Maps place exists (Knowledge Graph ID `/g/11y4zwq7dd`).
   - There is no Wikidata item for the lab or the founder, and no Justdial or Practo listing was found. One directory (Latrexa) lists the lab.
   - The PIN code appears three ways: **140302** (brochure and site), **140103** (Latrexa) and **140104** (Rayat Bahra University listings).
   - The Bahra University page that named EmbryoGenesis now redirects to the university homepage, so that citation is lost.
   - The ITHS site says the founder holds "15+ patents", which conflicts with the "patent application published" rule.
9. **The academy has a product gap, not a content gap.** No hands-on ICSI or andrology course in Punjab or Chandigarh surfaced; providers are in Indore, Mumbai, Gurugram, Hyderabad and Delhi. EmbryoGenesis's 8 lab courses do not cover those skills.

   Separately, aggregators list an **M.Sc. Clinical Embryology & Reproductive Genetics at Rayat Bahra University run with "Origin LIFE"**. The client should confirm whether this competes with the ITHS/EmbryoGenesis programme on the same campus.
10. **Ten new English posts, all inside the guardrails** (section 3):
    - Priority 1: receptivity tests explained (N3), endometrial biopsy (N4), chronic endometritis (N5), becoming an embryologist (N9) and embryology careers and salary (N10).
    - Priority 2: IVF-failure next steps (N1), RIF tests (N2), RPL tests (N6) and thin endometrium (N7).
    - Conditional: genital TB (N8), only if the client confirms TB-PCR is offered.
11. **AI baseline: 0 of 25.** EmbryoGenesis was cited in **0 of the 25 prompts** in the open proxy search.
    - It appeared once, and only when the search was restricted to its own domain (P6).
    - Its only third-party appearance is a directory listing (P24).
    - An indirect news mention exists: Himachal Tonite, 2024 (P19).

    Re-run the panel monthly in the five assistants.

## 8. Flags for the medical reviewer (Prof. Purkait, Dr. Vidhu Wadhwa)

1. **CD138 and chronic endometritis.**
   - *Human Reproduction* 2026 (Odendaal et al.) found CD138 is expressed in normal endometrium and concluded that "CD138-based CE testing and treatment should not be performed outside of a research context" ([eprint](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/239619)).
   - *Fertility and Sterility* 2022 (Ryan et al.) found plasma cells in 59.3% of follicular-phase biopsies and 19.7% of luteal-phase biopsies ([record](https://stanfordhealthcare.org/publications/858/858828.html)).
   - The site's sample window is day 21–23 (luteal).
   - **Decision needed:** how EIT is described, which markers it uses, which biopsy day suits it, and how to word its evidence status. ESHRE 2023 "can be considered" remains the formal guideline.
2. **Uterine NK testing.** ESHRE 2023: "Uterine NK cell testing is not recommended" ([PMC10270320](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10270320/)). The Endo-Immune Test and the EIP post need this status stated plainly.
3. **ERFA timing vs ERA timing.** The site gives HRT-cycle sampling at P+6 to P+8 and LH+7 to LH+9. The literature standard for ERA is P+5 / LH+7 ([PMC8045472](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8045472/)). N3 should explain why ERFA's window differs (a different method). Please confirm the wording.
4. **LIT and the Hindi post.** ESHRE RPL 2022 strongly recommends against LIT for unexplained RPL (report 02). The Hindi IVF-failure post says LIT "increases the chance of successful conception" and promotes PRP and stem cell therapy. This is for the client and legal adviser to decide (brief section 0 keeps old content).
5. **Founder descriptions.** "15+ patents" (ITHS site) and "patent application published" (guardrail 2) should be reconciled across all sites the group controls.
