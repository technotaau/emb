 ERMA                           Endometrial Receptivity & Maturation Analysis

Speciﬁc molecules, which we call implantation
biomarkers, are needed to present or absent at the
implantation site at the time of the “window of
implantation” (WOI). These biomarkers are
dysfunctional at the site of implantation and in the
endometrium of females who are suffering from RIF,
Recurrent Implantation Failure (RIF), and unexplained
infertility. The agonist biomarkers that facilitate the
attachment of embryo are called CAM (cellular
adhesion molecules). At the same time, speciﬁc
biomarkers that are antagonists to CAM are called anti-adhesion molecules. The females who
are suffering from Recurrent Implantation Failure (RIF) Recurrent Pregnancy Losses (RPL),
multiple miscarriages and unexplained infertility exhibit the presence of more anti-adhesion
molecules and absence of adhesion molecules. ERMA test assess the endometrium
development, maturation and presence or absence of implantation biomarkers that facilitate
embryo attachment especially at the time of Window of Implantation.

  CEF                             Chronic Endometritis Factor


Be it bacterial or viral; infection changes the environment
of the endometrium to the extent that it becomes
incapable of conception. A speciﬁc factor, which we call
chronic endometritis factor, is an indicator of
endometrial infection. In women with RPL (recurrent
pregnancy losses) (multiple miscarriages), RIF
(recurrent implantation failure (no attachment of the

very high. For implantation to occur, CEF needs to be
absent from the endometrium. CEF test ﬁnds out the
presence or absence of chronic endometritis in the
endometrium to rule out the infection in the endometrium
that hinders the implantation.

LIT THERAPY
Lymphocyte Immunization Therapy (LIT) represents an
innovative approach with the potential to help individuals
facing fertility challenges. This therapy involves the
extraction and processing of a patient's white blood cells
(leukocytes), which are then reintroduced into their body to
stimulate a speciﬁc immune response. By promoting
tolerance to the partner's sperm or embryos, LIT Therapy
aims to address immune-related infertility issues, such as
sperm antibodies or recurrent miscarriages. This
personalized treatment offers hope and the possibility of overcoming previously daunting reproductive
obstacles, ultimately increasing the chances of successful conception and realizing the dream of
parenthood.


                                                                              PBMC THERAPY
                                             Peripheral Blood Mononuclear Cells (PBMCs) present
                                             apromising avenue for individuals seeking assistance with
                                             fertility challenges. PBMCs, a type of white blood cell, have a
                                             pivotal role in regulating the immune system. In the realm of
                                             fertility, PBMCs ﬁnd application in immunomodulatory
                                             therapies designed to address issues like recurrent
                                             miscarriages or implantation failure, which have immune-
                                             related origins. By carefully modulating the immune response,
                                             PBMC-based treatments aim to create a more conducive
                                             environment for successful conception and pregnancy


PRP THERAPY
Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) therapy emerges as a promising
medical technique, showing potential
beneﬁts for couples facing fertility challenges. PRP entails the
extraction of a small volume of the patient's own blood, followed by
the concentration of platelets, which are then injected into the
reproductive region. This innovative approach is thought to boost
fertility by promoting tissue regeneration and enhancing blood
circulation to the reproductive organs. By revitalizing and fortifying
reproductive tissues, PRP holds the promise of improving the
likelihood of successful conception for individuals struggling with infertility. It represents a non-invasive,
secure, and increasingly popular choice within the realm of fertility treatments, providing fresh hope and
possibilities to those on their path to parenthood.

Stem Cell Therapy for Reproductive Health and Gynaecology Disorder Treatment

Stem cell therapy shows promise in the ﬁeld of reproductive health
and gynaecology disorders. In the realm of gynaecology disorders,
stem cell therapy presents a promising treatment avenue. Stem
cells' capacity to differentiate and repair damaged tissues has
generated interest in utilizing them for conditions affecting the
female reproductive system. Recent study demonstrates the
potential of human mesenchymal stem cell (hMSC) therapy in
premature ovarian failure (POF) and infertility, offering new
possibilities for managing issues related to hypoestrogenism and
primary ovarian insufﬁciency (Fu et al. 2021). The use of stem cells in
creating reproductive system organoids and treating infertility-related diseases, highlighting the role of
stem cell-based therapies in addressing male and female infertility issues (Jahan bani et al., 2021). Studies
emphasize the evolving landscape of regenerative medicine where the stem cell therapy provides
opportunities for revolutionizing the treatment of reproductive health disorders and collectively underscore
the signiﬁcant progress being made in utilizing stem cell therapy to management of gynaecology disorders
and improve reproductive health outcomes.



HYDROGEN THERAPY
Molecular Hydrogen therapy for reproductive health and oxidative stress management

Hydrogen is the lightest chemical element, non-toxic, colorless and
odorless gas. Considered for a long time a physiologically inert gas,
recent studies have demonstrated that hydrogen possesses
signiﬁcant antioxidant and anti-inﬂammatory properties that make the
use of this molecule potentially applicable in many different clinical
settings including Reproductive health, Reproductive Tissues,
Pregnancy, Neonatal Development and cancer treatment. Research
has shown that molecular hydrogen (H2) exhibits antioxidant effects in
normal human subjects and patients with diverse diagnoses such as
metabolic, rheumatic, cardiovascular, and neurodegenerative
conditions, as well as other diseases, infections, physical and radiation damage, ageing, and exercise-
related stress.
Hydrogen therapy has emerged as a promising avenue for enhancing reproductive health. Recent research
                                   highlights the potential beneﬁts of hydrogen therapies, such as oxy-
                                   hydrogen inhalation, in promoting reproductive health by acting as non-
                                   toxic antioxidants and anti-inﬂammatory compounds. Additionally,
                                   hydrogen peroxide, a crucial component in reproductive health, plays a
                                   signiﬁcant role in monitoring embryo health and diagnosing male
                                   infertility. Studies collectively suggest that hydrogen-based therapies
                                   and probiotics could offer novel and effective approaches to supporting
                                   reproductive health. Experimental use of hydrogen therapy has shown
                                   promise in reducing oxidative stress in both male and female
                                   reproductive systems. (Ref: Purdey et al., 2015 ; Russell & Nenov, 2022)
