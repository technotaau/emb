ABOUT US

Pioneering Hope and Innovation

We at EMBRYOGENESIS are committed to making the journey of people
struggling with infertility a road of hope and achievement. Our
infertility diagnostic centre is a leader in cutting-edge infertility
research and diagnostics, driven by a dedication to offering
individualized solutions to couples hoping to start or grow their
families.

Our Vision

To be the leading force in infertility diagnosis, where science,
compassion, and innovation converge to empower individuals and couples
on their path to parenthood. As the best fertility diagnostic centre in
India, we aspire to redefine possibilities, pioneer breakthroughs, and
unite hearts, making dreams of the family a tangible reality for all.

Who We Are

EMBRYOGENESIS is more than just an infertility diagnostic & research
centre; we are a community of passionate scientists, and healthcare
professionals who share a common mission: to make parenthood dreams a
reality. With years of experience, a state-of-the-art facility, and an
unwavering commitment to excellence, our diagnostic centre stands as a
beacon of hope for those seeking answers in their fertility journey.

IMMUNOTHERAPY: BALANCING YOUR IMMUNE RESPONSE

LIT – H2 ANTIOXIDANT THERAPY (Patented)

Lymphocyte Immunization Therapy (LIT) represents an

innovative approach with the potential to help individuals

facing fertility challenges. This therapy involves the

extraction and processing of a patient's white blood cells

(leukocytes), which are then reintroduced into their body

to stimulate a specific immune response. By promoting

tolerance to the partner's sperm or embryos, LIT Therapy

aims to address immune-related infertility issues, such

as sperm antibodies or recurrent miscarriages. This

personalized treatment offers hope and the possibility of

overcoming previously daunting reproductive obstacles,

ultimately increasing the chances of successful

conception and realizing the dream of parenthood.

PBMC THERAPY

Peripheral Blood Mononuclear Cells (PBMCs) present a promising avenue
for individuals seeking assistance with fertility challenges. PBMCs, a
type of white blood cell, have a pivotal role in regulating the immune
system. In the realm of fertility, PBMCs find application in
immunomodulatory therapies designed to address issues like recurrent
miscarriages or implantation failure,

which have immune-related origins. By carefully modulating the immune
response, PBMC-based treatments aim to create a more conducive
environment for successful conception and pregnancy.

REGENERATIVE THERAPY: REJUVENATE YOUR FERTILITY HEALTH

PRP THERAPY

Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) therapy emerges as a promising medical
technique, showing potential benefits for couples facing fertility
challenges. PRP entails the extraction of a small volume of the
patient's own blood, followed by the concentration of platelets, which
are then injected into the reproductive region. This innovative approach
is thought to boost fertility by promoting tissue regeneration and
enhancing blood circulation to the reproductive organs. By revitalizing
and fortifying reproductive tissues, PRP holds the promise of improving
the likelihood of successful conception for individuals struggling with
infertility. It represents a non-invasive, secure, and increasingly
popular choice within the realm of fertility treatments, providing fresh
hope and possibilities to those on their path to parenthood.

STEM CELL THERAPY

Stem Cell Therapy for Reproductive Health and Gynaecology Disorder
Treatment

Stem cell therapy shows promise in the field of reproductive health and
gynaecology disorders. In the realm of gynaecology disorders, stem cell
therapy presents a promising treatment avenue. Stem cells' capacity to
differentiate and repair damaged tissues has generated interest in
utilizing them for conditions affecting the female reproductive system.
Recent study demonstrates the potential of human mesenchymal stem cell
(hMSC) therapy in premature ovarian failure (POF) and infertility,
offering new possibilities for managing issues related to
hypoestrogenism and primary ovarian insufficiency (Fu et al. 2021). The
use of stem cells in creating reproductive system organoids and treating
infertility-related diseases, highlighting the role of stem cell-based
therapies in addressing male and female infertility issues (Jahan bani
et al., 2021). Studies emphasize the evolving landscape of regenerative
medicine where the stem cell therapy provides opportunities for
revolutionizing the treatment of reproductive health disorders and
collectively underscore the significant progress being made in utilizing
stem cell therapy to management of gynaecology disorders and improve
reproductive health outcomes.

ANTIOXIDANT FOR REPRODUCTIVE HEALTH AND OXIDATIVE STRESS MANAGEMENT

LIT- MOLECULAR HYDROGEN THERAPY

Hydrogen is the lightest chemical element, non-toxic, colorless and
odorless gas. Considered for a long time a physiologically inert gas,
recent studies have demonstrated that hydrogen possesses significant
antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties that make the use of this
molecule potentially applicable in many different clinical settings
including Reproductive health, Reproductive Tissues, Pregnancy, Neonatal
Development and cancer treatment. Research has shown that molecular
hydrogen (H2) exhibits antioxidant effects in normal human subjects and
patients with diverse diagnoses such as metabolic, rheumatic,
cardiovascular, and neurodegenerative conditions, as well as other
diseases, infections, physical and radiation damage, ageing, and
exercise-related stress.

Hydrogen therapy has emerged as a promising avenue for enhancing
reproductive health. Recent research highlights the potential benefits
of hydrogen therapies, such as oxy-hydrogen inhalation, in promoting
reproductive health by acting as non-toxic antioxidants and
anti-inflammatory compounds. Additionally, hydrogen peroxide, a crucial
component in reproductive health, plays a significant role in monitoring
embryo health and diagnosing male infertility. Studies collectively
suggest that hydrogen-based therapies and probiotics could offer novel
and effective approaches to supporting reproductive health. Experimental
use of hydrogen therapy has shown promise in reducing oxidative stress
in both male and female reproductive systems. (Ref: Purdey et al., 2015
; Russell & Nenov, 2022)
