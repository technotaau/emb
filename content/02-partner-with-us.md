## Section 2: What a collection partner is

**[H2]** What it means to be a collection partner

A collection partner is a hospital or IVF clinic that acts as an official collection point for EmbryoGenesis tests in its city.

Your clinicians take the endometrial sample for patients who need one of our tests. Your team packs it in our kit, completes the paperwork and hands it to our courier. We do the testing and send the report to the ordering clinician.

Partners are listed on our Collection Network page, so that other doctors in your area know where samples can be collected. {{CONFIRM}}

## Section 4: Who qualifies

**[H2]** Who can become a partner

We work with hospitals and clinics that can collect samples safely and follow our protocols. A partner usually has:

- **Registration.** A valid registration as an ART clinic under the ART (Regulation) Act 2021, or as a hospital or clinical establishment under the applicable state law.
- **A clinician who performs endometrial biopsy.** A gynaecologist or IVF specialist registered with the NMC or a State Medical Council.
- **Trained support staff.** At least one nurse, embryologist or lab technician who can pack, label and dispatch samples. We train them. {{CONFIRM}}
- **Bio-medical waste authorisation** under the Bio-Medical Waste Management Rules 2016.
- **Courier access.** A location that our courier can reach within {{MAX_TRANSIT_HOURS}} hours of collection. {{CONFIRM}}
- **Agreement to our standards.** Our collection SOP, patient-consent process and data-protection terms.

**Microcopy:** Not sure you qualify? Send an enquiry anyway. We will tell you what is needed.

## Section 6: How it works

**[H2]** How partnership works, step by step

**Step 1. Send an enquiry.** Fill in the short form below. Tell us about your centre and your city.

**Step 2. Introductory call.** A member of our scientific team calls you. We explain the tests, the evidence behind each one, and how collection works. You tell us about your patient mix and needs.

**Step 3. Documents and site check.** We ask for copies of your registrations and, where useful, a short site visit or video walk-through. {{CONFIRM}}

**Step 4. Partner agreement.** We sign a written agreement covering responsibilities, sample handling, data protection and fees for services.

**Step 5. Training and kits.** We train your team and deliver the first set of collection kits.

**Step 6. Go live.** You start collecting. We list your centre on our Collection Network page.

**Step 7. Ongoing support.** Your scientific contact stays available. We review sample quality and turnaround with you every {{REVIEW_FREQUENCY e.g. quarter}}.

## Section 8: Our commitments to partners

**[H2]** How we work with partners

- **No referral commissions.** We do not pay or accept commissions for patient referrals. Any fees in the partner agreement relate only to defined services such as sample collection, packing and dispatch. {{LEGAL_REVIEW}}
- **Clinical decisions stay with you.** Tests are ordered and interpreted by the treating clinician. We do not contact your patients to promote tests.
- **Honest information.** We never claim a test will improve pregnancy or IVF success rates. Our patient materials explain limits as clearly as uses.
- **Data protection.** Patient data is handled under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and our data-processing terms. Reports go only to the ordering clinician. {{CONFIRM_REPORT_TO_PATIENT_POLICY}}
- **No public prices.** We do not publish test prices. Partner pricing is shared privately.

## Section 10: Partner FAQ

**[H2]** Questions from clinics

**[H3]** 1. Which tests can partners collect for?
The Endometrial Infection Test (EIT), ERFA (The Soil Test), the Endo-Immune Test and Receptivity PX. {{CONFIRM_TEST_LIST}} Each needs an endometrial biopsy taken at a specific time. See the test menu for details.

**[H3]** 2. Who takes the sample?
A clinician at your centre who is registered with the NMC or a State Medical Council, following our collection SOP.

**[H3]** 3. Does our clinic need special equipment?
Usually not. Standard endometrial biopsy equipment is enough. We supply the kit for fixing, labelling and transporting the sample. {{CONFIRM}}

**[H3]** 4. How does the sample reach your lab?
Our courier collects it from your centre and brings it to our lab in Mohali. Each sample travels with its requisition form and a chain-of-custody record. {{CONFIRM_COURIER_ARRANGEMENT}}

**[H3]** 5. How quickly will we get reports?
Usually within 7 to 10 days of the sample reaching our lab. We will tell you if a sample needs repeat processing or if there is any delay.

**[H3]** 6. Who receives the report?
The clinician who ordered the test. Our scientific team is available to discuss the findings with them.

**[H3]** 7. What does partnership cost, and how are tests priced?
Partner pricing is in our rate card, which we share privately on request. We do not publish prices. {{CONFIRM_ANY_ONBOARDING_FEE}}

**[H3]** 8. Do you pay a referral fee?
No. We do not pay commissions for referring patients. Where a partner provides services such as collection, packing and dispatch, any fee for those services is written into the partner agreement.

**[H3]** 9. Is the partnership exclusive in our city?
{{CONFIRM_EXCLUSIVITY_POLICY}}

**[H3]** 10. How long does onboarding take?
About three to four weeks from first contact, depending on documents and training dates.

**[H3]** 11. What training do you provide?
Training on biopsy timing, sample handling, fixation, labelling, packing and paperwork. We can train on site or online. {{CONFIRM}}

**[H3]** 12. How do you handle patient data?
Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023. Our partner agreement includes data-processing terms. We use patient data only to perform the test and report it.

**[H3]** 13. Can we use EmbryoGenesis materials with our patients?
Yes. We provide scientifically reviewed patient leaflets, co-branded with your centre's name. Please do not change the clinical wording. {{CONFIRM}}

**[H3]** 14. What if a sample is rejected?
We tell the ordering clinician the reason the same day we find it, for example an unlabelled container or a delayed sample. A new sample may be needed. {{CONFIRM_REJECTION_POLICY}}

**[H3]** 15. Can our embryologists join your courses?
Yes. Our short hands-on courses are open to working lab staff. Partner staff may receive priority places. {{CONFIRM}}

**[H3]** 16. Can we leave the partnership?
Yes. The notice period and exit steps are set out in the partner agreement. {{CONFIRM_NOTICE_PERIOD}}
