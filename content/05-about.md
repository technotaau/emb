## Section 2: Our story

**[H2]** Our story

Many couples reach IVF clinics after one or more embryo transfers that did not work. Their doctors often want to look more closely at the lining of the uterus, where implantation happens. The specialised lab work this needs can mean sending samples to distant labs and waiting several weeks for results.

Prof. (Dr.) Pulakes Purkait set up EmbryoGenesis in Mohali in November 2023 to bring that work closer. Drawing on his background in molecular and reproductive genetics, the team developed in-house endometrial assays. Indian patent applications for two of them were published in 2024.

From the start, EmbryoGenesis has worked alongside IVF clinics rather than competing with them. Partner hospitals in Navi Mumbai, Udaipur and Jalandhar now collect samples for our lab. Alongside the lab, our academy trains clinical embryologists with university partners.

We are a young organisation. We say clearly what our tests can and cannot do, publish our work, and keep learning from the evidence.

## Section 3: Mission and vision

**[H2]** Mission and vision

**[H3]** Our mission
To give IVF clinicians across India timely, carefully reported endometrial and reproductive diagnostics, with an honest account of what each test can and cannot show.

**[H3]** Our vision
A fertility evaluation in India where every couple's doctor can access specialised endometrial testing close to home, choose tests on the basis of evidence, and work with well-trained embryology teams.

**[H3]** How we work
- **Evidence first.** We cite guidelines and studies, and we state the limits of every test.
- **Clinician-led.** Tests are ordered and interpreted by the treating doctor. We support, not replace, their judgement.
- **Timely.** Reports within 7 to 10 days of the sample reaching our lab.
- **Teaching as well as testing.** We train embryologists and clinic staff, and share what we learn through CME.
- **Respect for patients' data.** We handle personal and health data with care, under India's data-protection law.
