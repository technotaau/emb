    What the guidelines say

    ESHRE's 2023 good-practice recommendations on recurrent implantation failure state that testing for chronic endometritis can be considered, while routine endometrial receptivity testing and immune profiling are not recommended for routine use pending better evidence. We present each test with that status so you can decide case by case.

    References: ESHRE RIF good practice recommendations (2023); ESHRE RPL guideline (2022).

    Diagnostics · Endometrial Infection Test

    Endometrial Infection Test (chronic endometritis)

    A biopsy-based test for persistent, usually symptom-free inflammation of the uterine lining. Treatable when found; ESHRE says testing can be considered after repeated implantation failure.

    Sample: endometrial biopsyMethod: {{METHOD}}Report: 7–10 days

    Quick answer

    What it is: a lab test for chronic endometritis.
    Who orders it: your treating doctor.
    Who it may help: repeated IVF failure, recurrent pregnancy loss, unexplained infertility.
    What it is not: a treatment or a guarantee of pregnancy.

    Clinicians: how to order

    For clinicians

    IndicationsRIF after good-quality embryo transfer; RPL; unexplained infertility; suspected CE on hysteroscopy
    SampleEndometrial biopsy (pipelle), {{VOLUME}}
    Timing{{TIMING}} · avoid within 2 weeks of antibiotics
    Method{{METHOD e.g. CD138 IHC ± culture/PCR}}
    ReportPlasma cells per {{UNIT}}, interpretation, suggested follow-up per literature

    For patients

    Chronic endometritis rarely causes symptoms. Your doctor may suggest this test if good-quality embryos have not implanted or after more than one miscarriage. The biopsy takes a few minutes in the clinic. If positive, treatment is usually antibiotics followed by a repeat test.

    This test can tell you

    • whether plasma cells, a marker of chronic inflammation, are present
    • whether treatment has cleared them (on repeat)

    This test cannot tell you

    • whether a pregnancy will happen
    • whether your embryos are healthy
    • anything on its own; it is read with your full history
    Evidence status

    ESHRE's 2023 RIF recommendations state that testing for chronic endometritis can be considered. Observational studies associate treated CE with improved implantation in some cohorts; randomised evidence is limited.

    1. ESHRE Working Group on RIF, Hum Reprod Open 2023. 2. Cicinelli E et al., Hum Reprod 2015. 3. Kitaya K et al., Am J Reprod Immunol 2017. {{VERIFY}}

    Frequently asked

    Does the biopsy hurt?

    Most people describe cramping for a few minutes. Your clinic will advise on pain relief.

    Can the same biopsy be used for ERFA and the Endo-Immune Test?

    Yes. One sample can be split across the three panels when ordered together.

    How do I get this test?

    Ask your doctor. Tests are ordered by clinicians and processed through our collection centres.

    What does it cost?

    Your clinic or collection centre will quote the price. We do not publish prices online.
