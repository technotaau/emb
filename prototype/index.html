    OUR ADVANCED DIAGNOSTIC TESTS
    Test 1

    Endometrial Receptivity & Function Assay (ERFA – The Soil Test)

    What is ERFA?

    The ERFA, or Endometrial Soil Test, is a groundbreaking diagnostic tool that assesses the readiness of the uterine lining (endometrium) for successful embryo implantation. This comprehensive test analyzes multiple biomarkers to pinpoint factors affecting fertility and implantation success.

    Who is it for?

    • Individuals with unexplained infertility
    • Those who have experienced recurrent implantation failure (RIF)
    • Women facing repeated IVF failures or recurrent pregnancy loss (RPL

    Benefits:

    • Provides a full profile of endometrial receptivity
    • Explains hidden causes of implantation challenges
    • Guides personalized treatment strategies

    “Gain crucial insights into your fertility journey with ERFA – The SoiL Test.”

    Book Your Test Today
    Test 2

    Endo-Immune Test

    What is the Endo-Immune Test?

    This test delves into the immune landscape of the endometrium, essential for maintaining a balance between pro-inflammatory and anti-inflammatory responses. By evaluating cytokines and immune cells, we help identify potential immune-related issues causing recurrent miscarriages or failed implantation.

    Key Highlights:

    • Assesses immune competency to support healthy embryo implantation
    • Identifies imbalances in cytokine levels and uNK cell activity
    • Offers targeted insights for immune-modulatory treatments

    “Find answers to immune-related fertility challenges and explore tailored solutions.”

    Book your test today
    Test 3

    Endometrial Infection Test (EIT)

    Endometrial infections can go unnoticed but may severely impact fertility by altering the environment needed for successful implantation. The Endometrial Infection Test detects hidden bacterial or viral infections and chronic endometritis.

    Who Should Consider This Test?

    • Women with recurrent pregnancy loss (RPL)
    • Those experiencing repeated IVF failures or unexplained infertility

    Advantages:

    • Detects asymptomatic infections affecting fertility
    • Ensures a thorough assessment for a healthier endometrial environment

    “Ensure your endometrium is prepared for a healthy pregnancy with our EIT.”

    Immunotherapy: Balancing Your Immune Response

    LIT Therapy: Personalized Immune Support

    What is LIT Therapy?

    Lymphocyte Immunization Therapy (LIT) helps manage immune-related fertility challenges. This therapy involves the careful extraction and processing of your white blood cells (leukocytes), which are then reintroduced to stimulate a balanced immune response. By fostering tolerance to your partner’s sperm or embryos, LIT Therapy can help overcome issues like sperm antibodies and recurrent miscarriages.

    Key Benefits:

    • Promotes immune tolerance to support embryo implantation
    • Personalized approach tailored to each patient’s needs
    • Increases the likelihood of successful conception

    “Discover the personalized care that empowers your journey to parenthood.”

    PBMC Therapy: Immune System Enhancement for Conception

    What is PBMC Therapy?

    Peripheral Blood Mononuclear Cell (PBMC) Therapy leverages your body’s natural immune cells to create an environment conducive to successful conception. By adjusting immune responses, PBMC Therapy can help those experiencing repeated implantation failures or miscarriages.

    Key Benefits:

    • Regulates immune responses for better reproductive health
    • Supports the body in preventing immune-related fertility issues

    Want to find out if PBMC Therapy is right for you? Contact us today for more information.

    Regenerative Therapy: Rejuvenate Your Fertility Health

    PRP Therapy: Rejuvenating Fertility Health

    What is PRP Therapy?

    Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) Therapy involves the extraction of a small sample of your blood, concentrating the platelets, and injecting them into the reproductive region. This innovative, non-invasive treatment stimulates tissue regeneration and enhances blood flow, revitalizing reproductive tissues and boosting fertility potential.

    Key Benefits:

    • Stimulates natural tissue healing and regeneration
    • Enhances blood flow to reproductive organs
    • Non-invasive and safe, with minimal risk

    Embark on a journey filled with renewed hope. Schedule your consultation today!

    Stem Cell Therapy: Advanced Regenerative Medicine

    What is Stem Cell Therapy?

    Stem Cell Therapy is at the forefront of regenerative medicine, offering new hope for those facing gynecological disorders or infertility. Human mesenchymal stem cells (hMSCs) can differentiate and repair damaged tissues, making them invaluable for treating conditions like premature ovarian failure and primary ovarian insufficiency.

    Key Benefits:

    • Repairs and rejuvenates reproductive tissues
    • Offers solutions for both male and female infertility
    • Represents cutting-edge care in reproductive health

    Antioxidant For Reproductive Health And Oxidative Stress Management

    Molecular Hydrogen Therapy: Natural Antioxidant Support

    What is Molecular Hydrogen Therapy?

    Hydrogen therapy harnesses the power of this non-toxic, odorless gas to reduce oxidative stress and inflammation. Recent studies have shown hydrogen’s significant benefits in reproductive health, including enhanced tissue support and improved embryo quality.

    Key Benefits:

    • Acts as a powerful antioxidant and anti-inflammatory agent
    • Reduces oxidative stress, enhancing reproductive health
    • Supports overall wellness, contributing to successful conception
