How long is each training programme, and what is the fee?

Each of the eight hands-on training programmes at EmbryoGenesis, Mohali, runs for 1 to 5 Days with 10 seats per batch. Fees range from 1000/- for Biochemistry (Semi Auto Analyser, 1 day) to 35,000/- for Stem Cell Processing & Cryopreservation (1 day).
