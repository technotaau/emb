Immunotherapy: Balancing Your Immune Response

LIT Therapy: Personalized Immune Support

What is LIT Therapy?

Lymphocyte Immunization Therapy (LIT) helps manage immune-related fertility challenges. This therapy involves the careful extraction and processing of your white blood cells (leukocytes), which are then reintroduced to stimulate a balanced immune response. By fostering tolerance to your partner’s sperm or embryos, LIT Therapy can help overcome issues like sperm antibodies and recurrent miscarriages.

Key Benefits:

  • Promotes immune tolerance to support embryo implantation
  • Personalized approach tailored to each patient’s needs
  • Increases the likelihood of successful conception

PBMC Therapy: Immune System Enhancement for Conception

What is PBMC Therapy?

Peripheral Blood Mononuclear Cell (PBMC) Therapy leverages your body’s natural immune cells to create an environment conducive to successful conception. By adjusting immune responses, PBMC Therapy can help those experiencing repeated implantation failures or miscarriages.

Key Benefits:

  • Regulates immune responses for better reproductive health
  • Supports the body in preventing immune-related fertility issues

Regenerative Therapy: Rejuvenate Your Fertility Health

PRP Therapy: Rejuvenating Fertility Health

What is PRP Therapy?

Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) Therapy involves the extraction of a small sample of your blood, concentrating the platelets, and injecting them into the reproductive region. This innovative, non-invasive treatment stimulates tissue regeneration and enhances blood flow, revitalizing reproductive tissues and boosting fertility potential.

Key Benefits:

  • Stimulates natural tissue healing and regeneration
  • Enhances blood flow to reproductive organs
  • Non-invasive and safe, with minimal risk

Stem Cell Therapy: Advanced Regenerative Medicine

What is Stem Cell Therapy?

Stem Cell Therapy is at the forefront of regenerative medicine, offering new hope for those facing gynecological disorders or infertility. Human mesenchymal stem cells (hMSCs) can differentiate and repair damaged tissues, making them invaluable for treating conditions like premature ovarian failure and primary ovarian insufficiency.

Key Benefits:

  • Repairs and rejuvenates reproductive tissues
  • Offers solutions for both male and female infertility
  • Represents cutting-edge care in reproductive health
