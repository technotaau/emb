FAQs

What can I expect during these diagnostic tests?

Each test includes a comprehensive consultation, a simple collection process, and a detailed explanation of results. Our specialists ensure you feel informed and supported throughout.

How soon will I get my results?

Results are typically available within 7-10 days, depending on the complexity of the test. We prioritize accuracy and timely reporting to help you move forward confidently.

Will these tests help if I’ve already had IVF treatments?

Absolutely. Our diagnostic tests are particularly beneficial for those who have faced IVF failures or unexplained fertility issues. They provide deeper insights that standard tests may miss.
