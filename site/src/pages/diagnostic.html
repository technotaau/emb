Test 1

Endometrial Receptivity & Function Assay (ERFA – The Soil Test)

What is ERFA?

The ERFA, or Endometrial Soil Test, is a groundbreaking diagnostic tool that assesses the readiness of the uterine lining (endometrium) for successful embryo implantation. This comprehensive test analyzes multiple biomarkers to pinpoint factors affecting fertility and implantation success.

Who is ERFA for?

  • Individuals with unexplained infertility
  • Those who have experienced recurrent implantation failure (RIF)
  • Women facing repeated IVF failures or recurrent pregnancy loss (RPL)

What are the benefits of ERFA?

  • Provides a full profile of endometrial receptivity
  • Explains hidden causes of implantation challenges
  • Guides personalized treatment strategies

Test 2

Endo-Immune Test

What is the Endo-Immune Test?

This test delves into the immune landscape of the endometrium, essential for maintaining a balance between pro-inflammatory and anti-inflammatory responses. By evaluating cytokines and immune cells, we help identify potential immune-related issues causing recurrent miscarriages or failed implantation.

Key highlights of the Endo-Immune Test

  • Assesses immune competency to support healthy embryo implantation
  • Identifies imbalances in cytokine levels and uNK cell activity
  • Offers targeted insights for immune-modulatory treatments

Test 3

Endometrial Infection Test (EIT)

What is the Endometrial Infection Test (EIT)?

Endometrial infections can go unnoticed but may severely impact fertility by altering the environment needed for successful implantation. The Endometrial Infection Test detects hidden bacterial or viral infections and chronic endometritis.

Who should consider this test?

  • Women with recurrent pregnancy loss (RPL)
  • Those experiencing repeated IVF failures or unexplained infertility

Advantages of the EIT

  • Detects asymptomatic infections affecting fertility
  • Ensures a thorough assessment for a healthier endometrial environment
